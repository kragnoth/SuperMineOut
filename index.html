Core upgrades stack up to 8 per core and are inherited by every split: X Fission (splits now, then keeps budding on paddle hits, up to 8 cores), Strength, Pierce, Blast, Z arc, $ prospector. Broken ore is pulled up to your magnetic hopper, so be under it when it arrives. Every 60m there's an elevator junction: bank your cargo or push your luck.
